Error Analysis & Limitations
EA-01
Missing Data Bias
{{ metrics.total_neighbourhoods - metrics.high_count - metrics.medium_count - metrics.low_count }} neighbourhoods excluded from metrics due to missing temperature or ambiguous indicators. This may undercount high-risk areas in practice.
EA-02
Synthetic Data Limitation
All neighbourhood data is synthetically generated. Real-world correlations between temperature, built environment, and vulnerability may differ significantly. Baseline comparison should be validated with real historical data.
EA-03
Fixed Weight Assumption
Risk weights (Climate 35%, Vulnerability 30%, Built 20%, Service 15%) are domain-expert estimates. Actual weight optimisation using historical health outcomes would require real epidemiological data.
Key Findings
  • The neighbourhood-targeted approach identifies {{ metrics.high_count }} high-risk neighbourhoods that would be treated identically to low-risk areas under the baseline generic advisory.
  • High-risk communication reach improved from {{ metrics.baseline.high_reached_pct }}% (baseline) to {{ metrics.proposed.high_reached_pct }}% (targeted).
  • Missed high-risk areas reduced from {{ metrics.baseline.missed }} to {{ metrics.proposed.missed }}.
  • Communication timeliness improved from {{ metrics.baseline.timeliness }} to {{ metrics.proposed.timeliness }}.
  • The transparent scoring formula allows health officers to challenge and override any score — maintaining human accountability throughout.
